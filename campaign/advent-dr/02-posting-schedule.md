# 02 — Posting Schedule (manual first)

**Principle:** Day 1 is human-posted. No API automation until the language and
compliance are stable (see `04-...`). Do not publish anything until the
`00-FACTS.md` publish gate passes.

## Pre-flight (before Day 1)

- [ ] `00-FACTS.md` publish gate fully checked.
- [ ] Canva graphics A–D made, each carries the disclosure line.
- [ ] Copy blocks finalized with verified facts (no leftover `[BRACKETS]`).
- [ ] Confirm posting channels you actually control and that are connected.
- [ ] If MLS-backed: confirm the portal/MLS feed lane (`03-...`) so you don't
      double-post or violate feed rules.

## Manual scheduling tools (optional, free-tier aware)

- **Meta Business Suite** — schedule FB Page + Instagram posts/Reels/Stories at
  no cost. This is your primary scheduler for FB/IG.
- **Buffer (free)** — only if you need LinkedIn / X / Google Business Profile
  queued. Free plan is **3 channels** and **10 scheduled posts per channel** —
  enough for one listing, not a whole pipeline. Verify current limits before
  relying on them.
- **Later** — currently a **14-day free trial**, paid (Starter) after. Don't
  treat it as permanently free.
