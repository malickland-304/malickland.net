# 01 — Campaign Copy (adapt with verified facts from `00-FACTS.md`)

All copy uses `[BRACKETED]` fields. Replace them only with **verified** values.
Delete any line whose fact you cannot confirm — do not guess. Keep the
disclosure line on public posts/graphics. No fair-housing-sensitive language
(no targeting by or reference to family status, religion, race, national origin,
disability, etc.; no "safe," "perfect for families," "great schools," "exclusive
neighborhood").

Brand voice: professional, local, factual, direct. Brand colors for graphics —
Navy `#1e3a5f`, Gold `#c8961e`.
